Skill Capture turns work you already repeat into skills: written procedures a colleague
or an AI agent can follow, reviewed before anyone relies on them.

You do not write them. You work normally, and when you repeat something often enough you
are asked whether it is worth keeping.

## The shape of it

1. **The add-in watches the work.** In Excel, Word and PowerPoint it records which steps
   you took, in which order. Not your data — the steps.
2. **Repetition becomes a question.** Do this enough times and you get one prompt: save it,
   extend a skill that already exists, or branch your own version of one.
3. **A reviewer publishes it.** Drafts go to the reviewers for your area, who fix the
   wording and publish — or send it back with a reason.
4. **It starts small.** A new skill lives in the sandbox, visible only to your area. It
   moves to the firm, and later to the market, by passing gates, not by being popular.
5. **The map joins them up.** Skills that people do one after another become roads, so the
   system can answer "what comes next" rather than only "what exists".

## Five minutes well spent

- Open the front door and ask it something in your own words — a client, a schedule, a job.
  It answers with the thing and with where that thing sits in the work.
- Install the add-in. Nothing reaches the system until you do.
- Capture one worksheet you built yourself. It is the fastest way to see what the system
  actually understands, and the result is yours to correct.

## What it will not do

It will not publish anything on your behalf, move a skill up a stage without a human
approving it, or send your spreadsheet contents anywhere. Every prompt is refusable, and
saying no teaches it as much as saying yes.
