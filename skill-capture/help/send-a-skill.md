Skills move sideways as well as upward. You can send one — or several — straight to a
colleague without promoting it to the firm first.

## How a transfer works

1. **You offer.** The skills are pinned by their content hash at the moment you offer, so
   what arrives is exactly what you sent.
2. **Both legs go into escrow.** Yours is the skill; theirs, if it is priced, is the money.
3. **They cross together, or not at all.** If anything has changed on either side, the
   transfer refuses and the credits are refunded. Nothing moves one way.

## Free and paid

Free transfers are the common case: a colleague needs your way of doing something today.
Priced transfers exist so that the person who did the work of making something reusable can
be paid for it by the people who save time using it.

## Forwarding

By default the person you send to can use the skill, not pass it on. Allow forwarding
explicitly if you mean them to be able to.

## What the recipient gets

The skill at the exact version you sent, in their library, attributed to you. The transfer
is written into the provenance chain, so months later both of you can prove what was sent,
when, and that it has not changed since.
