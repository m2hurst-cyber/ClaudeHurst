# Engage the client and open the return

## When to use

At the start of every individual return, new client or returning. Nothing is prepared until the engagement letter is signed.

## Inputs

- The client's request and contact details
- Last year's federal and state returns (yours or the prior preparer's)
- The engagement letter template and the individual tax organizer (resources)

## Steps

1. Run a conflict check and confirm the work is in scope (an individual return, plus any state returns).
2. Prepare the engagement letter: tax year, returns covered, what the client must provide, fees, deadlines, and that the client is responsible for the accuracy of their information.
3. Send the letter and the organizer through the secure portal. Never ask for Social Security numbers, bank details or documents by plain email.
4. Verify the client's identity for a new client (government ID, matched to the prior-year return).
5. Get the prior-year return and note carryovers: capital losses, passive losses, charitable contributions, credits, overpayment applied to this year.
6. Confirm filing status as of December 31, dependents (relationship, age, residency, support) and changes during the year: marriage, divorce, birth, death, move.
7. Walk the client through the situation questions in the organizer. Each "yes" adds its branch to the route.

## Outputs

- Signed engagement letter on file
- Organizer with personal details, filing status, dependents and situation answers
- The route for this return: the mainline plus a branch for each situation that applies

## Checks

- Don't start preparing until the engagement letter is signed.
- Filing status and dependents are decided on the facts at year end; document how you decided.
- If an answer is "not sure", treat it as "yes" until the documents show otherwise.
