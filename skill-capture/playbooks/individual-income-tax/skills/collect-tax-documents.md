# Collect the tax documents

## When to use

After the engagement is signed and the situation questions are answered.

## Inputs

- The organizer answers and last year's return (each prior-year form should appear again unless something ended)
- The document request checklist (resource)

## Steps

1. Start the request list from last year's return: every employer, bank, brokerage, lender and payer that issued a form.
2. Add the documents each "yes" situation needs (for example 1099-NEC and business records for self-employment, 1099-B for investment sales, 1095-A for Marketplace insurance).
3. Always request: W-2s, 1099-INT/DIV/B/R/G/SSA, 1098 mortgage interest, 1098-T, property tax bills, charitable receipts, childcare provider details, records of estimated payments made, and any IRS or state notices.
4. Send the list through the secure portal with a due date. Most forms arrive by mid-February; K-1s and corrected 1099s often come later.
5. Mark each document received, and compare against the list weekly. Chase what's missing; note anything the client says they won't have.
6. Watch for corrected forms (marked "CORRECTED") and replace the original.

## Outputs

- A complete set of source documents, or a dated list of what's still missing and why

## Checks

- Every payer on last year's return is either on this year's list or explained (account closed, job ended).
- Documents are stored in the client's secure folder, never in email.
- If a needed document won't arrive before the deadline, plan an extension (branch: file an extension).
