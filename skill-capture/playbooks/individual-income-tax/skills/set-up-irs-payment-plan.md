# Set up an IRS payment plan

## When to use

Branch after "Pay the tax" when the client can't pay in full by the due date.

## Inputs

- The balance due, what the client can pay now, and a monthly amount they can sustain

## Steps

1. File the return on time anyway: the failure-to-file penalty is far larger than the failure-to-pay penalty.
2. Pay as much as possible by the due date to reduce penalties and interest.
3. Choose short-term (paid within months) or a long-term installment agreement; apply through the client's IRS online account (Online Payment Agreement) or Form 9465. States have their own plans.
4. Prefer direct debit for the installments; record the agreement terms and dates.
5. Make sure next year's withholding or estimates are enough, since a new balance can default the plan.

## Outputs

- An accepted payment plan with its schedule and terms on file

## Checks

- The client knows penalties and interest continue until the balance is paid.
- Next year's estimates are planned so the agreement stays in good standing.
