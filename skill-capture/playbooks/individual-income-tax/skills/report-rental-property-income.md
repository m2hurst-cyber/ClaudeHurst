# Report rental property income (Schedule E)

## When to use

Branch after "Report income" when the client had rental property.

## Inputs

- Rent received, expense records (mortgage interest, taxes, insurance, repairs, management fees), the depreciation schedule, days rented and days of personal use

## Steps

1. Total rents and expenses per property; separate repairs (deductible) from improvements (depreciated).
2. Update the depreciation schedule for each property and any improvements placed in service.
3. Count days rented at fair rent and days of personal use; apply the vacation-home rules when personal use is significant.
4. For short-term rentals, check whether average stays and services provided change the reporting.
5. Apply passive activity loss rules (Form 8582) and any carryover of suspended losses.
6. Rejoin the mainline with net rental income or allowable loss.

## Outputs

- Schedule E per property with an updated depreciation schedule and passive loss carryover

## Checks

- Depreciation continues from last year's schedule; no property drops off.
- Suspended losses carried forward are recorded for next year.
