# Prepare the return

## When to use

When income, deductions and credits are worked through.

## Inputs

- Workpapers, the organizer, the prior-year return and carryovers

## Steps

1. Roll forward the client in the tax software (or create them) and confirm identity details against the prior-year return.
2. Enter income, adjustments, deductions, credits, payments and withholding from the workpapers.
3. Prepare the resident state return, and branch for multi-state or foreign situations.
4. Clear every software diagnostic; document any you override and why.
5. Reconcile: total income, withholding and estimated payments in the return equal the workpapers.
6. Compare the result to last year and explain big swings in tax, refund or balance due.
7. Note the balance due or refund, and whether the client wants a refund applied to next year.

## Outputs

- A complete draft federal and state return, ready for review, with a prior-year comparison

## Checks

- No unexplained diagnostic overrides.
- Estimated payments match the IRS record when the client is unsure (IRS online account).
- Bank details for direct deposit or debit are taken from a voided check or bank letter, not retyped from email.
