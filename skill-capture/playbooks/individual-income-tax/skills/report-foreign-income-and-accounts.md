# Report foreign income and accounts

## When to use

Branch after "Prepare the return" for any foreign income, foreign tax or foreign account.

## Inputs

- Foreign pay slips, foreign tax returns or assessments, foreign bank and investment statements with the year's highest balances

## Steps

1. Convert foreign income and taxes to US dollars using a consistent, documented rate.
2. Choose between the foreign tax credit (Form 1116) and the foreign earned income exclusion (Form 2555) when the client lives abroad; consider carryovers.
3. FBAR: if the total of foreign account balances exceeded $10,000 at any time in the year, file FinCEN 114 separately with FinCEN by its deadline.
4. Form 8938: check the specified foreign financial assets thresholds for the client's filing status and residence.
5. Rejoin the mainline.

## Outputs

- Foreign tax credit or exclusion on the return, and FBAR and Form 8938 filed where required

## Checks

- FBAR is filed with FinCEN, not with the return.
- Exchange rates and sources are documented.
