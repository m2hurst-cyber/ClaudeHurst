# Report investment sales (Form 8949, Schedule D)

## When to use

Branch after "Report income" when the client sold investments or crypto.

## Inputs

- Consolidated 1099-B statements, crypto exchange reports, records of inherited or gifted assets, last year's capital loss carryover

## Steps

1. Import 1099-B transactions into the software; keep the broker's summary for tie-out.
2. Where basis wasn't reported to the IRS (noncovered lots, inherited, gifted, or transferred assets, most crypto), get the basis from the client's records and document it.
3. Check holding periods (short- versus long-term), wash-sale adjustments and any sales of a main home.
4. Apply last year's capital loss carryover and the annual capital loss limit; carry the rest forward.
5. Answer the digital asset question on Form 1040 accurately.
6. Rejoin the mainline with net capital gain or loss.

## Outputs

- Form 8949 and Schedule D tied to the 1099-B totals, with basis support for anything not reported

## Checks

- Proceeds equal the 1099-B totals.
- No sale shows zero basis unless that's really right.
- The carryover to next year is recorded.
