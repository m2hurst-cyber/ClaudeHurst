# Claim the Health Savings Account (Form 8889)

## When to use

Branch after "Claim deductions and credits" when the client has an HSA.

## Inputs

- W-2 box 12 code W (employer and payroll contributions), Form 5498-SA, Form 1099-SA, months of high-deductible health plan coverage

## Steps

1. Confirm months covered by a high-deductible plan and self-only or family coverage.
2. Add up contributions (employer plus personal) and compare to this year's limit for that coverage and the client's age; fix excess contributions before the deadline.
3. Deduct personal contributions not made through payroll.
4. For distributions (1099-SA), confirm they paid qualified medical expenses; otherwise they're taxable and may carry a penalty.
5. Rejoin the mainline.

## Outputs

- Form 8889 and the HSA deduction

## Checks

- Contributions don't exceed this year's limit.
- The client keeps receipts for medical expenses paid from the HSA.
