# Pay the tax

## When to use

When the return or extension is filed. Payment is due by the original deadline even when the return is extended.

## Inputs

- The final balance due (federal and each state) and the due date
- The client's bank details, confirmed during review

## Steps

1. Confirm the amount and the due date for each return.
2. Choose how the client pays: electronic funds withdrawal scheduled with the e-file, IRS Direct Pay or the client's IRS online account, EFTPS, debit or credit card (with a processor fee), or a check with Form 1040-V. States have their own options.
3. Schedule the payment for no later than the due date and give the client the date and amount in writing.
4. Record the confirmation number and date in the client file.
5. For a refund, record the expected deposit and follow up if it hasn't arrived in three weeks.
6. If the client can't pay the full amount, take the payment plan branch.

## Outputs

- A confirmed payment (or tracked refund) for every return

## Checks

- Payment confirmation is on file for each jurisdiction.
- Late payment means penalties and interest; tell the client before the due date, not after.
