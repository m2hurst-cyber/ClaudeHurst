# Claim education credits (Form 8863)

## When to use

Branch after "Claim deductions and credits" when there's a 1098-T or tuition paid.

## Inputs

- Form 1098-T for each student, receipts for required books and materials, scholarships and grants, 529 plan distributions (1099-Q)

## Steps

1. Work out qualified expenses per student: tuition and required fees and materials, less tax-free scholarships and grants.
2. Check which credit each student qualifies for (years of study, half-time enrollment, no felony drug conviction for the American opportunity credit) and the income limits.
3. Coordinate with 529 distributions so the same expenses aren't used twice.
4. Pick the better credit per student and complete Form 8863.
5. Rejoin the mainline.

## Outputs

- Form 8863 with each student's credit and the expenses supporting it

## Checks

- The student isn't also claimed on another return for the same expenses.
- The school's EIN from the 1098-T is on the form.
