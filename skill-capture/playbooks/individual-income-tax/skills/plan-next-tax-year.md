# Close and plan next year

## When to use

After filing and payment.

## Inputs

- The filed return and the estimated tax worksheet (resource)

## Steps

1. Decide whether the client needs estimated payments next year (income without withholding, or a balance due this year). Use the safe-harbor rules in the current Form 1040-ES instructions.
2. Set the quarterly amounts and dates (generally April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15) and any state estimates.
3. If the client is a W-2 employee, suggest a W-4 change instead of, or as well as, estimates.
4. Deliver copies of the filed returns, the payment schedule and payment vouchers through the portal.
5. Archive the workpapers and the signed 8879. Keep records at least as long as the IRS can assess (generally three years, longer in some cases).
6. Send the closing note and diary next year's engagement.

## Outputs

- A next-year payment schedule, delivered copies, and a closed, archived engagement

## Checks

- The client has every due date in writing.
- Carryovers to next year are noted where next year's preparer will see them.
