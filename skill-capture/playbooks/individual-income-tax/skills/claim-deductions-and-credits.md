# Claim deductions and credits

## When to use

After income is complete. Deduction and credit situations the client answered "yes" to are branches after this step.

## Inputs

- Income totals and the client's deduction records: mortgage interest (1098), property and state taxes, charitable receipts, medical expenses, childcare costs

## Steps

1. Adjustments to income (Schedule 1, Part II): educator expenses, deductible IRA contributions, student loan interest, self-employed health insurance and retirement contributions, HSA contributions.
2. Itemized deductions (Schedule A): state and local taxes (subject to the cap), mortgage interest, charitable gifts (with receipts), medical expenses above the AGI floor. Use this year's limits from the IRS instructions.
3. Take the larger of itemized and standard deductions, and record why.
4. Credits: child tax credit and credit for other dependents, child and dependent care (Form 2441), education (branch), earned income credit, premium tax credit (branch), energy credits.
5. Check that each dependent claimed meets the tests for each credit claimed.

## Outputs

- Adjusted gross income, the deduction taken, and the credits claimed, each with support

## Checks

- Charitable gifts of $250 or more have a written acknowledgment.
- Credits with eligibility questionnaires (such as the earned income credit) have the due diligence documented.
- Use the current year's limits; never last year's.
