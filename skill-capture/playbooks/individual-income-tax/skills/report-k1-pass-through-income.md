# Report K-1 pass-through income

## When to use

Branch after "Report income" when the client received a K-1. K-1s often arrive late; plan an extension if one is missing near the deadline.

## Inputs

- Every K-1 with its footnotes and state schedules; last year's basis and carryover records

## Steps

1. Enter each K-1 box by box, including footnotes and supplemental statements; don't summarize.
2. Update the client's basis (and at-risk amount) in each entity: contributions, income, losses, distributions.
3. Apply basis, at-risk and passive loss limits in that order; carry disallowed amounts forward.
4. Carry state-source income to the multi-state branch if the entity operates in other states.
5. Rejoin the mainline.

## Outputs

- K-1 items on the return, and an updated basis schedule per entity

## Checks

- Distributions above basis are reported as gain.
- Every K-1 received is entered; a missing K-1 means an extension, not an estimate.
