# Generate Schedule K-1s

## When to use

After the allocations are confirmed. Partners need K-1s in time to file their own returns; a late K-1 usually means an extension for the partner.

## Inputs

- Confirmed Schedule K totals and partner allocations

## Steps

1. Generate one K-1 statement per partner: each Schedule K amount times the partner's profit percentage, plus the partner's own distributions.
2. Round to the cent so each line across all K-1s adds up exactly to the return (the largest remainders take the extra cents).
3. Tie the K-1 totals back to the Form 1065, line by line.
4. Enter or compare the K-1s in the tax software, which produces the official Schedule K-1.
5. Deliver each K-1: send it into the partner's individual return on this platform, or through the secure portal.

## Outputs

- One K-1 statement (PDF) per partner, tied to the 1065, and a delivery record

## Checks

- K-1 totals equal the return's Schedule K on every line.
- Every partner received their K-1; corrected K-1s replace the originals in the partner's return.
