# Allocate partnership items to partners

## When to use

After the partnership's Form 1065 is prepared in the tax software, before any K-1 is issued.

## Inputs

- The Schedule K totals from the prepared Form 1065
- The partnership agreement and each partner's capital account, with any changes in ownership during the year

## Steps

1. Tie the Schedule K totals (ordinary income, rental income, interest, dividends, distributions) to the books and the prepared return.
2. Confirm each partner's profit, loss and capital percentages from the agreement; for ownership changes during the year, use the agreement's method (interim closing or proration) and document it.
3. Check that profit and capital percentages each add up to 100%, and that distributions to partners add up to the return's.
4. Note special allocations and guaranteed payments, which are not allocated by percentage; handle them in the software.

## Outputs

- A confirmed allocation per partner, ready for the K-1 generator

## Checks

- Percentages add up to 100%; distributions tie to the return.
- Every partner is identified by a reference, never by TIN outside the tax software.
