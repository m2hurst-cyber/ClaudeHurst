# Survey the roof and the site

## When to use

On the site visit for every job. Everything downstream (takeoff, price, crew, dumpster, safety plan) comes from this sheet.

## Inputs

- The measurement and condition report (resource)
- Ladder, lift or hatch access arranged at qualification
- Camera, moisture meter and core cutter as the system needs

## Steps

1. Measure each roof plane: length, width, pitch. Record them separately; one total hides the valleys, hips and ridges that drive labour.
2. Count the linear work: ridge, hip, valley, rake, eave, wall flashing, counter-flashing, coping.
3. Record the existing system: covering, number of layers, insulation, deck material and thickness, fastening.
4. Record the deck condition honestly, including where you could not see it. Note soft spots, ponding, prior repairs and anything that suggests rot underneath.
5. List every penetration: vents, stacks, curbs, skylights, HVAC units, drains, scuppers, conduit, existing solar standoffs.
6. Record access and hazards: setup space, overhead lines, fragile surfaces, tie-off points, parking, dumpster position, lift requirement, occupied space below.
7. For solar, add what the array needs: unshaded area, azimuth and tilt per plane, structural framing seen, main panel location, service size and conductor route.
8. Photograph in a fixed order (each plane, every penetration, each defect, the access) and index the photos to the plane they belong to.
9. Note what you could not inspect, and what would change the price if it turns out worse.

## Outputs

- Measurements by plane with pitch, totalled into squares
- Condition and penetration list, access and hazards
- An indexed photo set
- Named assumptions and the unknowns behind them

## Checks

- Measure; don't estimate from the ground. Note where a measurement is from imagery rather than a tape.
- Say what you could not see. The hidden-damage branch exists because of exactly those lines.
- If the deck condition is unknown, the proposal needs a unit price for deck replacement, not silence.
