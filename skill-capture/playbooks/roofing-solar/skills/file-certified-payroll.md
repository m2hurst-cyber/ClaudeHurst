# File the certified payroll

## When to use

Every week work is performed on a job subject to prevailing wage, from the first week to the last.

## Inputs

- The wage determination attached to the contract
- Time records by worker, day, classification and hours
- The awarding body's filing system and format

## Steps

1. Read the wage determination on this contract and list the classifications it covers, with their base rates and fringe amounts.
2. Classify each worker by the work they actually performed, not by their job title. Split hours where someone worked in two classifications.
3. Check that paid rates meet or exceed base plus fringe, however your company delivers fringes (cash or benefit plans).
4. Record apprentices with their programme, ratio and step, and check the ratio was held on site.
5. Build the payroll for the week: worker, classification, hours by day, rate, gross, deductions and net, in the required form.
6. Sign the statement of compliance by someone authorised to sign it.
7. File by the deadline, in the system the awarding body uses, and keep the confirmation.
8. Keep the underlying time records and the fringe evidence; an audit asks for those, not just the forms.
9. Fix and refile promptly if an error is found. Late or wrong filings can hold the whole payment.

## Outputs

- Weekly certified payroll filed on time, with the compliance statement signed
- Classification and apprentice ratio record
- Supporting time and fringe records kept

## Checks

- Classification by the work done is the part auditors challenge most.
- Payment is often withheld while a certified payroll is missing, so file even for a week with no work if the body requires a nil report.

## Notes

Prevailing wage rules, forms, filing systems and deadlines differ by jurisdiction and contract and change over time. Follow the determination and the awarding body's current instructions.
