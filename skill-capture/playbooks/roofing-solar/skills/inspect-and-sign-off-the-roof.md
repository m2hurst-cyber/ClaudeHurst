# Inspect, correct and sign off

## When to use

As each inspection point is reached, and at substantial completion before the final billing.

## Inputs

- The permit's inspection schedule and conditions
- The manufacturer's inspection requirement for the warranty you promised
- The daily log and photo record

## Steps

1. Book each inspection at the stage the permit names, and have the right person on site to walk it.
2. Record the result the same day: passed, passed with conditions, or failed with the correction notice.
3. Clear corrections quickly, photograph the correction, and get the re-inspection signed.
4. Book the manufacturer's inspection if the system warranty requires one, and keep its report.
5. Walk the job with the customer and write the punch list together: item, location, who does it, by when.
6. Close each punch item with a photograph and the date.
7. Get substantial completion acknowledged in writing. It usually starts the clock on retainage and on your warranty term.

## Outputs

- Every required inspection recorded and passed
- A punch list, closed item by item with evidence
- Substantial completion acknowledged in writing

## Checks

- Don't bill the final payment while a required inspection is outstanding.
- A punch item without a date is an open argument.
- Keep the inspection reports with the closeout packet; the warranty may depend on them.
