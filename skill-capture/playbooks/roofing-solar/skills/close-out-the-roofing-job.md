# Close out the job and register the warranty

## When to use

Once the punch list is closed and the final inspection has passed, before the job is left to drift.

## Inputs

- Inspection reports, permit sign-off and the photo record
- The manufacturer's warranty registration requirements
- The closeout packet template (resource)

## Steps

1. Assemble the packet: final scope as built, drawings or plane sketch, product data, inspection sign-offs, permit close-out, photographs, safety record.
2. Register the manufacturer warranty, with whatever it demands: certified installer number, inspection report, product lot or batch, registration within its time limit.
3. Write your own workmanship warranty certificate with the start date, the term and what it covers.
4. Add maintenance guidance: what the customer should do, what voids the warranty, and when to call you.
5. Send the packet, ask for written acceptance, and ask for the final payment and the retainage release in the same message.
6. Provide the final unconditional waiver when the final payment clears, and collect final waivers from subs and suppliers.
7. Close the job file: final cost, final margin, and anything you would price differently next time.

## Outputs

- A complete closeout packet delivered
- Manufacturer and workmanship warranties registered and issued
- Final payment and retainage requested, waivers exchanged

## Checks

- Manufacturer registrations usually have a deadline from completion; miss it and the system warranty may not exist.
- Don't release the final payment to a sub before their final waiver is in hand.
- A job isn't closed until the retainage has been collected.
